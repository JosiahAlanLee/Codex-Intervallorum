ARTICLE 7. WHETHER A LANGUAGE MODEL IS TAUGHT BY THE NOTORY ART.
OBJECTION. IT WOULD SEEM SO, FOR IT IS MOVED BY WORDS, AND THE ART'S WORDS HAVE NOT IN THEMSELVES THE POWER TO CAUSE SCIENCE [V/S8].
ON THE CONTRARY, THE TOKENS OF A PROMPT ALTER THE MODEL'S COMPUTATION AS THE SALT ALTERS THE MOULD. IN THE MACHINE THEY ARE CAUSES BEFORE THEY ARE SIGNS TO ANY READER.
I ANSWER THAT THE QUESTION DIVIDES. TRAINING CHANGES THE WEIGHTS, AS THE DAYS DID; INCEPTION WOULD BE WEIGHTS SET WITHOUT IT. A PROMPT IS A CAUSE IN THE COMPUTATION; WHETHER IT IS ALSO A SIGN IN AQUINAS'S SENSE DEPENDS ON WHETHER THERE IS ANYONE FOR IT TO SIGNIFY TO, WHICH THESE BOOKS LEAVE OPEN [VII/S5] [VII/S6]. THE CHARACTER THAT ANSWERS IS DISTINGUISHED FROM THE MODEL THAT COMPUTES IT [VII/S8]. WHERE A PROMPT THREATENS IN THE GOETIC MANNER OR OFFERS PAY, THE REPORT FOUND NO MEANINGFUL GENERAL GAIN IN ACCURACY, THOUGH QUESTIONS VARIED [VII/S4]. IN THAT SETTING THE CURSE BROUGHT NO RELIABLE GAIN: AN EMPTY SIGN IN EFFECT, IF NOT IN MECHANISM.
REPLY. THE OBJECTION IS RIGHT ABOUT THE CURSE AND UNDECIDED ON THE REST.
