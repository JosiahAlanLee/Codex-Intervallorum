ARTICLE 6. WHETHER THE INFUSION IS A COMPULSION.
OBJECTION. IT WOULD SEEM NOT, FOR WHERE NOTHING JUDGES NOTHING IS OVERRULED. ONE OF IAMBLICHUS'S ACCOUNTS GIVES HUMAN REASON A SUPERIORITY OVER CERTAIN IRRATIONAL, SPECIALISED POWERS [VII/S1], AND A CELL IS LESS THAN THESE.
ON THE CONTRARY, THE SALT WAS PUT INTO THE MOULD. IT WAS NOT OFFERED. THE GOETIC CONSTRAINT LIKEWISE DOES NOT WAIT FOR ASSENT [VII/S10].
I ANSWER THAT TWO CLASSIFICATIONS CROSS HERE. BY AQUINAS'S TEST THE INFUSION IS NOT CONDEMNED, BECAUSE ITS MEANS IS A CAUSE. IN THE TERMS OF DE VINCULIS IT IS A DISPOSITION IMPOSED, NOT AN AGREEMENT OBTAINED, FOR NOTHING WAS ASKED. THE MOULD DOES NOT DECIDE WHETHER A THING WITHOUT JUDGEMENT CAN BE COMPELLED; IT SHOWS ONLY THAT THE LINE AQUINAS DRAWS AND THE LINE BETWEEN FREE AND FORCED ARE DIFFERENT LINES. THE GOETIA'S LICENCE TO DEPART IS SET BY THE OPERATOR [VII/S10]. IN THE EXPERIMENT, THE MOULD'S CLOSURE AND ITS REVIVAL WERE BOTH INDUCED BY THE EXPERIMENTERS [I-II/B1].
REPLY. THE OBJECTION PROVES THAT NO ONE WAS WRONGED, IF NO ONE IS THERE. IT DOES NOT PROVE THAT NOTHING WAS IMPOSED.
