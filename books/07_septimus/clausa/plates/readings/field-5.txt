ARTICLE 5. WHETHER THIS INFUSION IS THE NOTORY ART THAT AQUINAS CONDEMNS.
OBJECTION. IT WOULD SEEM SO, FOR IT SEEKS KNOWLEDGE WITHOUT STUDY, AND THAT IS THE ART AQUINAS FORBIDS [V/S8].
ON THE CONTRARY, AQUINAS DOES NOT CONDEMN THE ART FOR ITS SPEED. HE CONDEMNS IT BECAUSE ITS MEANS HAVE NOT IN THEMSELVES THE POWER TO CAUSE SCIENCE: IT USES THEM AS SIGNS, NOT AS CAUSES, AND AS SIGNS NOT INSTITUTED BY GOD, SO THAT THEY BELONG TO A COMPACT WITH DEMONS [V/S8].
I ANSWER THAT SALT IS A CAUSE AND NOT A SIGN. IT ACTS ON THE MOULD BY WHAT IT IS, NOT BY WHAT IT MEANS, AND NO ONE NEED BE ADDRESSED FOR IT TO ACT. BY AQUINAS'S OWN TEST THE INCEPTION FALLS OUTSIDE HIS CONDEMNATION. HIS SECOND JUDGEMENT, THAT THE ART IS FUTILE BECAUSE, NOT SEEKING KNOWLEDGE IN THE WAY CONNATURAL TO MAN, IT MUST LOOK TO GOD OR TO DEMONS [V/S8], FAILS FOR THE MOULD: THE ROUTE IS NOT ITS OWN, BUT THE SALT ACTS BY ITS OWN NATURE, AND NEITHER GOD NOR DEMON IS ASKED. THE ONLY WORKING NOTORY ART IN THESE BOOKS IS NOT A NOTORY ART.
REPLY. THE FAULT AQUINAS FINDS IS IN THE MEANS, NOT IN THE SPEED.
