ARTICLE 2. WHETHER WHAT THE MOULD LEARNS IS KEPT IN A HIERARCHY.
OBJECTION. IT WOULD SEEM NOT, FOR A HIERARCHY IS A SACRED ORDER THAT PASSES ILLUMINATION DOWN FROM RANK TO RANK [IV/H1], AND THE MOULD HAS NO RANKS.
ON THE CONTRARY, KRAMAR AND ALIM FIND THE PLACE OF FOOD WRITTEN INTO A HIERARCHY OF TUBE DIAMETERS, WHICH THEN SHAPES LATER TRANSPORT AND MIGRATION [I-II/B2].
I ANSWER THAT THE MOULD HAS A HIERARCHY THAT NO ONE COMMANDED. WHAT THE FLOW CARRIES WIDENS SOME TUBES, AND OTHERS SHRINK; THE WIDE TUBES CARRY THE NEXT FLOW. THE SLOWER LEVEL HOLDS WHAT THE FASTER LEVEL DID AND CONSTRAINS IT, AS SLOWER LEVELS CONSTRAIN FASTER ONES [IV/A1]. THE ORDER IS WRITTEN UPWARD BY WHAT PASSED THROUGH IT, NOT DOWNWARD BY WHAT STANDS ABOVE IT.
REPLY. THE OBJECTION DEFINES HIERARCHY BY ITS DIRECTION. THE MOULD KEEPS AN ORDER OF GREATER AND LESSER WITHOUT ANYONE TO HAND IT DOWN.
