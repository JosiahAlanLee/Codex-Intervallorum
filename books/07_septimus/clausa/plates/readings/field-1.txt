WHETHER KNOWLEDGE MAY BE INFUSED INTO A SLIME MOULD WITHOUT STUDY. IN SEVEN ARTICLES. EVERY KEY IN BRACKETS IS IN THE END BIBLIOGRAPHY OR IN THE REGISTER OF THE SEVENTH BOOK. THE SLIME MOULD RESULTS ARE TAKEN FROM [I-II/B1] AS ITS AUTHORS' INSTITUTION REPORTED THEM; ITS FULL METHODS WERE NOT READ.

ARTICLE 1. WHETHER A SLIME MOULD CAN LEARN.
OBJECTION. IT WOULD SEEM NOT, FOR LEARNING BELONGS TO WHAT HAS NERVES, AND THE PLASMODIUM OF PHYSARUM POLYCEPHALUM IS ONE CELL.
ON THE CONTRARY, BOUSSARD, DELESCLUSE, PEREZ-ESCUDERO AND DUSSUTOUR REPORT THAT THIS CELL BECOMES HABITUATED TO SALT [I-II/B1].
I ANSWER THAT LEARNING IS TAKEN HERE ONLY IN THE SENSE THE EXPERIMENT TESTS: AN AVERSION THAT WEAKENS AFTER REPEATED EXPOSURE. IN THAT SENSE IT LEARNS. WHETHER THERE IS ANYONE IN THE CELL TO WHOM THE LEARNING HAPPENS IS NOT ASKED HERE, AND NOTHING BELOW ANSWERS IT.
REPLY. THE OBJECTION NAMES AN ORGAN WHERE IT SHOULD NAME AN OPERATION.
