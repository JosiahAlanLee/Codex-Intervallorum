# Editorial record

## Divergence

Working title: **Clavicula Intervallorum**, *A Little Key to the Intervals*.
The earlier title and edition remain in upstream Git history. This is a new
interior argument and reading order, not a silent revision of Revision 5.

- Removed the Borrowed Face section, its foldout, and the Force Majeure / Vis
  Maior tract from the active source tree and all outputs.
- Rebuilt the book pipeline with repository-relative paths, local fonts,
  ordinary SVG, and ReportLab. No original canvas runtime or external blobs.
- Replaced the original plate sequence with twelve new vector compositions.
  The Second Neck deliberately develops XI's motif. The original XI remains
  only as the input to its independently portable plotter exporter.
- Made the packet's three preservation targets, separate clocks, material
  carriers, maintenance supports, selective abeyance, and recovery dimensions
  the sequence's organizing material.
- Corrected the outage discussion: event timestamps do not establish causal
  partitions into organizational and physical delay.
- Did not convert the earlier six-case pilot into new evidence. Its comparison
  materials were unavailable in the repository.
- Kept values distinct from calculations. No physical or biological result
  establishes standing, care, or legitimate authority.

## Voices and entertainment

Each plate includes a short invented deposition. The recurring errors are
clerical: counting a vessel rather than its contents, entering an inherited
constraint twice as independent evidence, confusing a route on paper with
one a traveler can take, or describing a supported refuge as costless.

The formal hand, red intervention, and incomplete trial field remain separate.
All twelve marks have keyed readings. No hidden doctrine is required to read
the work. Ornamental hatchwork and pin rings carry no dependency semantics.

The E/D/H/V/F tags are a deliberate tradeoff: they make the interior's status
auditable but interrupt a pseudo-historical illusion. The diagrams and fictional
depositions carry the manuscript conceit; the reading leaves keep the argument
explicit. They can be restyled later without weakening the distinction.

## Next editorial experiments

1. A physical two-color artist's edition of II, V, VI, and VIII. These need
   plate-specific conversion of fills, hatch density, lettering, and gaps.
2. A set of numbered workshop slips presenting genuine alternative readings
   of the same diagram. They should disagree about evidence or goals, not only
   add decorative marginalia.
3. A second empirical specimen only after its comparison materials can be
   bundled with the result. A general theory is not a prerequisite for the art.

## Rights and distribution

The new repository is intended as a separate development home. No public
release or project-wide license is chosen on the author's behalf. Font notices
are bundled. Authorship and source lineage remain explicit.
