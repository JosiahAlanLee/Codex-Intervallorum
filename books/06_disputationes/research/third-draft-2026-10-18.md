## Decisions that remain the author's

1. **The [V] proposals.** Accept, amend or refuse the institutional commitments of IV and the participation rule of IX. Nothing here adopts them; on adoption their tag stands as the key defines it.
2. **Title and numbering.** *De Potestate et Accusatione* and D15 to D19 are proposals. The Clavis does not list the new claims.
3. **Placement.** The swap with the Tarot, its binder, validator and text changes, and where De Matribus Noctis stands; listed in the README. *Settled later the same day in the development repository, and in this edition on 19 October: the author asked for the swap, and the book is bound as Book VI with De Matribus Noctis before it; see `docs/POTESTATE-2026-10-19.md`.*
4. **The plates' sentences.** Each plate's [F] sentence and hidden falsifier was written for this draft and can be changed in `plates/tabulae.py`.
5. **Plotting.** The plot masters are untested on a physical plotter.

## Limits that stay

The reading of the Mitsubishi decision against D16 is this book's, not the court's. The booked ledger is the model's accounting assumption, and D18 asks for records the model cannot supply. The case record that suggested D19 cannot test it. Selection by record, D17, has one historical instance and no modern test.
