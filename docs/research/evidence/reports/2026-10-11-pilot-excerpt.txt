 Expanded incident pilot: results
 11 October 2026 | Revisable Systems Practice, Revision 5

 The data are more useful for examining correction mechanisms. An
 incremental advantage of the protocol remains unestablished. The run
 completed source-located manual coding, ten paired analytical readings, nine
 timestamp calculations, and sensitivity counts. It did not execute the independent
 three-arm model experiment prepared in the earlier package. No fresh-context
 inference endpoint or independent judging was used; the same analyst already knew
 the case outcomes. The numerical output is reproducible aggregation of that analyst's
 annotations, not an automated or blinded diagnosis.


 Scope and version
 Ten units were inspected: T-Mobile 2020, Orange 2021, BT 2023, Fastly 2021, OVH
 2021, Cloudflare 2023, Alibaba Cloud Hong Kong 2022, Anthropic's 2026 assessment
 cluster, GTG-1002 in 2025, and OpenAI-Hugging Face 2026. The main denominator is
 eight new, non-originating units. Cloudflare was in the first pilot. Hugging Face
 informed the original concept. Anthropic's four related incidents are one source cluster,
 not four independent replications. Organizations and source publishers also repeat
 across units.

 Revision 5 is current and was read in full. The original Revision 3 coding definitions are
 retained only for directly comparable fields. D1-D5 are assessed separately. Selection
 and source screening occurred before the analysis contract; neither this run nor its
 newly defined fields is preregistered. The cases are retrospective and purposively
 selected, not a representative incident sample.


 Reproduction and access limitations
 Run python3 run.py to validate the ledger and regenerate results/summary.json.
 The script checks identifiers, evidence locators attached to positive codes, subgroup
 membership, and nine interval calculations. It cannot independently verify the truth of
 source statements or the analyst's causal judgments. The artifact is an executed
 manual pilot with reproducible bookkeeping.

 Source references and precise locators are in cases.json; the PDF provides clickable
 reference links. The FCC technical report was accessible only through primary indexed
 passages in this run. The French and Chinese passages were translated by the analyst.
 No full third-party reports or offensive payloads are reproduced. Source claims remain
 attributed claims. The complete user-owned Revision 5 text is included to fix the
 protocol version.

 The more defensible logging model is: signal observed; problem identified; decision

 overlap. Record authority as a causal explanation only when evidence identifies it,
 rather than labeling all pre-restoration time organizational delay.


 Paired application
 The case ledger contains a competent domain reading, a protocol question and an
 explicit boundary for all ten units. Reliability reviews include human factors,
 coordination and learning; agent cases use security and alignment review rather than a
 technical-only uptime baseline. No independent A/B/C outputs or judges exist in this
 run, so these readings are not a measured null effect.

 The most concrete result is that the new evidence improves the constraints on an
 interpretation. Orange distinguishes escalation from supplier engagement; BT
 distinguishes a decision from successful execution; OVH exposes a limit on
 investigative access; Alibaba distinguishes a destination from a usable recovery path.
 The source authors already identify the relevant mechanisms. Recasting them as
 correction or bypass failures does not establish a new discovery.

 The agent material permits questions that the original hosting sample could not
 address. Anthropic reports perturbations of model inputs and differences between
 expressed assessments and behavior. That is useful evidence about a technical system
 responding to correction. It does not supply the institutional longitudinal series
 demanded by D4, and its training analysis does not justify a general claim that
 observed biased behavior was consistently reinforced. [AN1]


 Revision 5's distinctive claims
 D1, decay ownership: retained traces can support reconstruction, but these cases do
 not compare otherwise similar networks under actual expiration versus third-party
 retention. No comparative test is identified.

 D2, temporal legibility: no matched change between predictable and unpredictable
 timing at fixed frequency is available. Calendar dates and overnight response do not
 establish strategic timing effects.

 D3, identity-lesson separation: no measured reporting penalties, identification
 policy, reporting volume and learning outcomes are jointly available. Publicly
 anonymized prose is not proof of a protective internal reporting regime.

 D4, directional corrigibility: several incident paths can be partially reconstructed,
 but they do not yield paired longitudinal power/correction series. Model sensitivity to a
 changed cue is also not the same variable as an institution acting on a substantiated
 report. Prediction of later serious failures is untested.

 D5, displacement: two limited coding trials, BT's procedures and Alibaba's cooling
 operation, produce the same provisional partial vector: mission retained, structure
 modified, other axes unknown. They concern different actors and endpoints, so they
 are not ranked or averaged. No complete vector or comparative recovery benefit is
 established. Treating every reconfiguration as revision would make the code
 uninformative. In adversarial cases, preservation of an attacker's ability to reorganize
 can also worsen the defender's outcome; the actor and welfare endpoint must be
 declared first.

 All five distinctive comparative claims therefore remain untested by an adequate
 design. This is a finding about the selected evidence, not five falsifications of the
 claims. Revision 5's predicted lack of benefit on mature reliability reviews is not scored
 as a successful prediction: the independent comparison needed to measure a null
 benefit was not executed.


 What was learned and what remains runnable
 The operational reading can be made more precise, and the new documents expose
 factual disagreements and explicit decision points. The evidence does not yet show
 that the protocol finds supported mechanisms that a competent equal-length review
 misses. It supports building stronger evaluation packets, not declaring efficacy.

 For an actual three-arm trial, freeze the expanded protocol adaptation and an equally
 detailed generic checklist; use the same domain-competent baseline and identical
 source packets in isolated contexts; cap findings and output length; and obtain
 independent, condition-masked judging. Score actionable supported findings,
 unsupported additions, and review burden separately. These already-inspected cases
 are development material. Anonymizing names would not make them unseen or
 remove model familiarity. Prospective prediction also requires independently dated
 pre-incident records, not a timeline reconstructed from the final report.

 Do not broaden the interpretation to national regulatory quality. This run has no
 matched jurisdictions, workload denominator or measure of supervisory capacity.
 Mainland China reserve sources were not silently substituted for the Hong Kong
 operator report, and a thin public disclosure is not evidence that no investigation
 happened.
