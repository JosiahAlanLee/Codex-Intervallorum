# Source ledger

Access accounts for the public evidence review, prepared 18 October 2026. Direct passages, abstracts, excerpts and secondary summaries have different scopes; the descriptions below preserve those limits. The institutional programme pages supplementing this ledger are listed in the review. No study is represented as independently reproduced.

- **S02.** Woolley et al. (2010), [Evidence for a collective intelligence factor in the performance of human groups](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20929725/), *Science* 330:686–688, DOI 10.1126/science.1193147. Abstract inspected. The existence of scalar operationalization is used; no claim that its factor structure is uncontested or exhausts intelligence.

- **S04.** Ashby (1956), [An Introduction to Cybernetics](https://ashby.info/Ashby-Introduction-to-Cybernetics.pdf), chapter 11, especially 11/5 onward. Relevant primary passage inspected. No new theorem about commensurable institutional growth rates is attributed to it.

- **S05.** Gelman and Shalizi (2013), [Philosophy and the practice of Bayesian statistics](https://sites.stat.columbia.edu/gelman/research/published/philosophy.pdf), *British Journal of Mathematical and Statistical Psychology* 66:8–38. Opening and data-analysis-cycle discussion inspected. The disagreement is methodological; the reviewed argument does not invalidate Bayesian computation.

- **S06.** GitLab (2017), [Postmortem of database outage of January 31](https://about.gitlab.com/blog/postmortem-of-database-outage-of-january-31/), 10 February. Recovery and corrective-action passages inspected. Operator self-report; not a causal ownership experiment.

- **S07.** NASA ASRS, [Confidentiality and Incentives to Report](https://asrs.arc.nasa.gov/overview/confidentiality.html). Page inspected. Used for the existence and design of de-identification and qualified protections, not a numerical causal efficacy claim or individual legal advice.

- **S08.** Heylighen (2016), [Stigmergy as a universal coordination mechanism I: Definition and components](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1389041715000327), *Cognitive Systems Research* 38:4–13, DOI 10.1016/j.cogsys.2015.12.002. Indexed publisher abstract inspected; full opening failed. Used for the stated conceptual definition, not a general empirical law about politics.

- **S09.** Elish (2019), [Moral Crumple Zones](https://estsjournal.org/index.php/ests/article/view/260), *Engaging Science, Technology, and Society* 5:40–60. Abstract inspected for the concept; individual accident reconstructions not freshly audited.

- **S11.** Brynjolfsson, Li, and Raymond, [Generative AI at Work](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.11771), version 2, 6 November 2024. Research abstract inspected. Its measured support-work outcomes are not assumed to transfer to visual artists, every workplace, or every later model.

- **S12.** Simon (1962), [The Architecture of Complexity](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/course/15-440/assets/READINGS/simon-architecture-of-complexity-1962.pdf), *Proceedings of the American Philosophical Society* 106:467–482. Primary scan downloaded; printed pp. 474–475 inspected through OCR because the scan lacked usable extracted text. No universal rule of managerial legitimacy is inferred.

- **S13.** Meta (2021), [More details about the October 4 outage](https://engineering.fb.com/2021/10/05/networking-traffic/outage-details/), 5 October. Access, security, and recovery discussion inspected. Operator account illustrating an actual competing objective, not proof that every access restriction is necessary.

- **S14.** Butlin et al. (online 2025; journal issue 2026), [Identifying indicators of consciousness in AI systems](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41219038/), *Trends in Cognitive Sciences* 30:488–501, DOI 10.1016/j.tics.2025.10.011. Abstract and publisher highlights inspected. Theory-derived indicators are a research method, not a verdict on the book's models.

- **S15.** Lennon (2026), [How Seriously Should We Take AI Welfare? Constraints From the Epistemology of Consciousness](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/phpr.70148), *Philosophy and Phenomenological Research*, published online 13 July. Abstract inspected for the stated epistemic objection; no endorsement of its full argument is implied.

- **S16.** Anthropic (2025), [Emergent introspective awareness in LLMs](https://www.anthropic.com/research/introspection), 29 October. Main findings and limitations inspected. Vendor research with bounded experiments; accurate reporting of some injected internal states does not establish phenomenal experience or general reliability of self-report.

- **S17.** Doshi and Hauser (2024), [Generative AI enhances individual creativity but reduces the collective diversity of novel content](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10195027/), *Science Advances* 10:eadn5290. Author-repository abstract inspected. Its short-story experiment is not a study of professional illustrators or the present book.

- **S18.** LaMarre, Landreville, and Beam (2009), [The Irony of Satire](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1940161208330904), DOI 10.1177/1940161208330904. Indexed publisher abstract inspected; full opening timed out. Used for observed variation in interpretation, not a numerical forecast for this artwork.

- **S19.** Kim, Garg, Peng, and Garg (2025), [Correlated Errors in Large Language Models](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/kim25e.html), ICML, PMLR 267:30038–30066. Primary abstract inspected. Its tasks differ from manuscript review; no numerical error-correlation estimate is transferred to these reviewers.

- **S20.** Carter (2025; online 2023), [Work, Gender and Witchcraft in Early Modern England](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/1468-0424.12717), *Gender & History* 37:91–108. Primary text's scope and work/contact sections inspected. Accusation formation in these records is not a measure of execution patterns across Europe.

- **S25.** Freeman, [The Tyranny of Structurelessness](https://www.jofreeman.com/joreen/tyranny.htm), author's composite text of published versions. Relevant informal-power and democratic-structuring passages inspected. Movement analysis and proposals, not a proof that formalization cures domination.

- **S26.** Hamraie and Fritsch (2019), [Crip Technoscience Manifesto](https://catalystjournal.org/index.php/catalyst/article/download/29607/24772?inline=1), *Catalyst* 5(1):1–33. Interdependence, access, and disability-justice sections inspected. Normative and historical argument; its use here supplies no endorsement of a particular AI provider or ownership regime.

- **S27.** Benson, Li, and Shue (2019), [Promotions and the Peter Principle](https://carlsonschool.umn.edu/sites/carlsonschool.umn.edu/files/2019-08/peterprinciple.pdf), author-hosted version dated 29 July. Abstract inspected directly. The sampling frame and promotion outcomes concern sales workers and their managers; no estimate is transferred to NOCs or all hierarchies.

- **S28.** Bronfenbrenner (2009), [No Holds Barred: The Intensification of Employer Opposition to Organizing](https://ecommons.cornell.edu/entities/publication/35b4d7d7-a2ea-4e18-90d4-4cf756e4514b). Author-repository research excerpt inspected; linked full PDF opening failed. Its source design is used to distinguish documented campaign opposition from a universal theory of hierarchy's purpose. Contemporary law or the present prevalence of tactics is not inferred from the historical study.

- **S31.** Ostrom, Janssen, and Anderies (2007), [Going beyond panaceas](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17881583/), *PNAS* 104:15176–15178, DOI 10.1073/pnas.0701886104. Indexed primary abstract inspected; direct full opening was blocked. Its resource-governance scope is preserved; no quantitative result about nationalizing AI labs is supplied.
