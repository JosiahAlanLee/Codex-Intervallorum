# Scapegoating Research

This folder holds research toward a Witch(x) account of scapegoating inside organizations, added on 18 October 2026 for review. None of it has been applied to the manuscript. Where it would go in the book is not decided.

## For review

1. Metz, gap 5: whether the hundred-franc fine punished the false accusation (Morley), holding her without a judge's licence (Prost), or both. The Latin of Ep. II.46, *Opera* vol. 2, p. 763, could clarify what Brennonius reports and has not been read; it need not settle the court's actual grounds.
2. Metz, the record: everything reaches us through Agrippa's papers. Brulé (2006), pp. 69–71, is the likeliest route to a court or chapter record.
3. The register's **Reading:** lines are interpretation rather than the text's claim. Check whether any overreaches.
4. The register's second pressure point: CULPAX's single timestamp (III:518) depicts the attribution failure that I–II:215 and IV:279 propose investigating. Which incident records could establish that an institution used that defective rule?
5. The cases: whether Mitsubishi Fuso's hubs holds on the primary record, and whether Horizon or the 737 MAX does better.
