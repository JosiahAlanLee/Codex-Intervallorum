# Book V: Ifá source-to-claim check

22 October 2026. Reviewing instance: ChatGPT/Codex. Public manuscript base: `b387b256e298d190e31504ebb118b638a1644999`, especially “Reading leaf — what the index leaves out,” “The borrowed test,” and N4. This record assesses the borrowing; it does not implement an Ifá divination procedure.

**Editorial decision:** retain the five evidence-route labels as an independent Codex proposal inspired by the comparison. A distinction between firsthand experience and testimony does not entail those five categories, their use across every discipline, or their practical effectiveness. N4 already proposes a comparison to test that effectiveness. Source verification does not discharge that test.

“Verified” below means that an inspected source supports the specified attribution, or that the finite calculation follows from its premises. It does not certify the truth of reported divinatory outcomes. “Contradicted” identifies a formulation rejected by the inspected text. “Interpretation-dependent” marks this review's assessment or a generalization exceeding the passage. “Still inaccessible” means the required pages were not obtained through the routes checked, not that no lawful copy exists.

IV–V (pp. 40–59, including the ranking tables and exceptional selection cases), Abimbola's procedural chapter beginning p. 13, and the 2020 book's chapters titled “What Is Ifa?”, “Knowledge in Ifa,” “How Is Knowledge Acquired in Ifa?” and “How Is Knowledge Verified in Ifa?” Collate actual passages across editions before changing their status.
