# De Potestate et Accusatione — placement as Book VI, 19 October 2026

At the author's request, this pass binds the third discussion draft of *De Potestate et
Accusatione* as Book VI in both editions, in place of the Tarot of Intervals. The same change
was made in the development repository, `JosiahAlanLee/Librum_Quinque`, on 18 October. It
does not revise the book's argument, adopt its [V] proposals, or claim a fresh audit of its
sources.

## What it is

The book holds three powers apart that often travel together: authority over production,
exposure to harm and exposure to blame. It reads a witchcraft accusation at Metz in 1519,
the Wells Fargo and Mitsubishi wheel-hub cases, the OpenAI and Hugging Face incident and the
opening books' gate-and-window model, then turns the same questions on the volume's own
production. Five claims, D15–D19, continue the series' numbering. Nine native vector plates
each stand on a verso facing the opening of their section; four carry no data, five are drawn
from the records they name. See [the book's folder](../books/06_disputationes/README.md).

## Placement

Book VI, between De Matribus Noctis and De Vinculis, in both editions (`bind.POTESTATE`).
The Tarot of Intervals is kept as a separate volume: its sources and builder are unchanged,
`scripts/build.py` still builds it, the components artifact still carries it, and Book III's
illustrated setting still takes its Foras figure from the Tarot art.

The binder treats Book VI as it treats the parables and Septimus. Its reading leaves 1–47
keep their local folios and page sides. Its register (leaves 48–56) is reset in the one end
bibliography under the `VI/` prefix, and its preparation leaf (57) moves to the production
notes under *VI / preparation*, after Matres and before VII. The author's name and the
edition line leave the title leaf for those notes, as on every other book's title leaf.

Book VI's bracketed keys link to its register. `unified.relink`, which now also serves
`relink_septimus`, points each of its 42 key links at the consolidated entry. A Book VI link
covers the key alone, so the link box can catch part of a bracket or a comma; the binder reads
the key through that and requires it to head an entry on the standalone register leaf the
link pointed to. An internal link it cannot read stops the build.

## The register in the series' form

The book's citations use the series' form (surname and initials, year, title, venue), so its
entries sort among the others in the one alphabetical bibliography. No bibliographic fact was
added in rewriting them from the second draft's full-name form.

Consolidation joins five Book VI entries to other books': AHRQ's primer with VII/S19, I-II/Q1
and IV/Q1 (identical citations); DORA's change-approval page with I-II/Q3 and IV/Q3, and
Freeman's composite version with I-II/G3 (new `SAME_SOURCE` groups, since the books cite
them in different words); Elish's article with III/D05 and IV/EL1 (an existing rule); and the
labour critique with Acc/R2 (identical citation). Each book's wording and account stay with
its key. A `SAME_SOURCE` group replaces the identical-citation merge for its members, so it
must name every key that cites the source; a first version of the DORA group named only
I-II/Q3 and split it from IV/Q3. A new unit test fails if two entries share a citation. Nine
of the twenty-six Book VI entries are project records and follow the literature as a
separate list.

## What this edition carries and what it does not

The book's text, register, plates code, builder, validator and third-draft record are here.
Its plates and PDF are generated by the build and are not committed, as for every other book.

- **The second draft and its records.** ChatGPT/Codex's second draft, its source ledger and
  its revision record were filed as private editorial records. They stay in the development
  repository; the first public pass remains preserved in pull request 5. The register's
  accounts still say which sources were inspected for the second draft and by whom.
- **The incident case record.** Tabula V draws the record's timeline and codings at build
  time. Its data file, `case.json`, is vendored unmodified in `plates/vendor`, with its
  source commit and hash in the plates README. The record's README and validator are not
  part of this selection; its quoted book passages are those of the development repository
  at that commit. The register (VI/I4) says so.
- **The red-team review.** The labour critique the book answers (VI/B3, merged with Acc/R2)
  is cited, not included, as the [migration record](MIGRATION.md) excluded the earlier
  red-team reviews. Its account says so.
- **The gate-and-window record.** This edition revised the model's README and report on
  18 October and omitted the closing passage that handed the "routed to people who cannot
  remove it" premise to the scapegoating inquiry. The Proem now says that its *first* record
  ended on that premise, and VI/M2 names the version read. `results.csv` is byte-identical,
  and the figures in Book VI's fifth section recompute from it with this edition's
  `verdict.py`.

## De Matribus Noctis

It was placed before the Tarot as the threshold to the volume's largest body of figures,
the seventy-nine cards. It keeps that place, now before Book VI. The reason has changed: the
parable answers one charge against the volume's figures and leaves the others standing, and
Book VI's eighth section takes up the production charge and qualifies the parable's answer:
"The absence of one primordial original does not erase the antecedents actually used."
Moving it remains a one-line change to `bind.MATRES`'s position in the two `BOOKS` lists; the
placement is the author's to revisit.

## Validation

`binding/validate_typeset.py` now requires, for Book VI, a build manifest that matches the
bound source; every register and preparation leaf outside the body; nine vector plates on
versos facing sections that name them; every key link at its consolidated entry and no other
internal link; and De Vinculis immediately after it. The Tarot checks remain for that volume
if it is bound again. Four unit tests cover the register's collection and order, key links
read through brackets, an unreadable link stopping the build, and one entry per citation.

Built and checked for this pass: `scripts/build.py`'s components with their tests and
validators, Book VI's plot masters and reading plates, and, after the DORA fix, both editions
with the binding validator and the project check. The reading edition runs to 373 pages with 200 bibliography
entries, Book VI at 223–269; the illustrated edition, after scribal and vellum finishing, to
377 pages with the same entries, Book VI at 227–273. The binding tests number 34. The figures
in Book VI's fifth section were recomputed with this edition's `verdict.py`: 0.996 and 0.750
for the bounded gate, 0.607 for the universal gate's holding criterion, 0.722 on the booked
ledger, and 3,118 cells, 738 of them anchored, where the two ledgers disagree.

## Files

`books/06_disputationes/` holds the book. `binding/bind.py` and `binding/bind_illustrated.py`
list it; `binding/backmatter.py` reads its register (`BOOK_VI_REGISTER`) and joins two
citations to I-II's; `binding/unified.py` moves its preparation leaf, re-links its keys and
words the note on the binding; `binding/validate_typeset.py` checks it. `scripts/build.py`
draws and validates its plates and builds and validates the book before both editions; the
workflow uploads it with the components.
